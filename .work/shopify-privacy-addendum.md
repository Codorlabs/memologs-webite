# Shopify privacy and merchant processing terms

## Objective

Publish the authorized free Shopify analytics privacy/data-processing terms for MEMO AI, Inc. and connect them to the existing Privacy Policy and Terms. The user prioritized app submission and authorized manual privacy fulfillment while deletion/provenance automation is completed later.

## Acceptance criteria

- A public, readable Shopify-specific page identifies the legal entity/address/contact, free report, minimal data and coverage, limited purposes, excluded advertising/shared training/external AI, actual hosting provider, retention, manual or automated rights fulfillment, security and incident duties.
- General terms and privacy policy link the specific terms and give them precedence for Shopify data. Other products' pricing and terms remain scoped to those products.
- Use confirmed United States Google Cloud billing to identify Google LLC. Do not claim executed SCCs, vendor ZDR, company certifications or external AI enablement.
- Describe current 15-backup/seven-day PITR recovery settings without pretending that the earlier proposed seven-day backup target is implemented.
- Verify the production build and rendered public pages, then use the normal website publication path. Do not claim the backend controls are live without separate deployment verification.

## Non-Goals

- Editing unrelated website redesign work, changing general governing law, changing cloud retention or enabling new AI processors.
- Representing an unexecuted transfer instrument as signed or claiming automated erasure is complete.

## Verification

Build Next.js, inspect the rendered routes and navigation, and verify the published page before updating the Shopify questionnaire.
